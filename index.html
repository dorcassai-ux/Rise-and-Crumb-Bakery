    Our story

    Rise & Crumb started in a home kitchen with one oven and a jar of sourdough starter. Today we are still a small team, still mixing by hand, and still baking everything the same morning we sell it.

    We use local flour where we can, real butter, and no preservatives. If it is not fresh, it does not go on the shelf.

    • 4 a.m. first oven lit
    • 48 hrs sourdough fermentation
    • 0 preservatives
